How much does HealthLabs.com cost?
Tests start at around $29 for basic panels and range up to $300+ for comprehensive hormone or allergy testing. Prices are clearly displayed upfront with no hidden fees. Are HealthLabs.com results accurate?
Yes, results are just as accurate as tests ordered by doctors because they use the exact same CLIA-certified laboratories (Quest Diagnostics and LabCorp). The accuracy depends on the specific test, but all labs follow strict federal quality standards.
Will my insurance company find out?
No. Since you're paying directly and not using insurance, there is no reporting to insurance companies, employers, or your doctor (unless you choose to share). Your results are completely private and accessible only through your secure online account.
How do I prepare for my lab test?
Preparation depends on the specific test. Some tests require fasting for 8-12 hours, while others have no special requirements. HealthLabs.com provides detailed preparation instructions for each test when you order. Bring your lab order form and a valid ID to the testing location.
