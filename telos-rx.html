Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tirzepatide and how does it work?
Tirzepatide is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist that works by mimicking natural hormones to regulate appetite, slow gastric emptying, and improve insulin sensitivity. This powerful combination leads to significant weight loss by reducing hunger, increasing satiety, and optimizing metabolic function. Clinical trials have shown up to 22% body weight reduction over 72 weeks.
How much weight can I expect to lose?
Clinical studies demonstrate that Tirzepatide can produce 15-22% body weight reduction over 12-18 months. Individual results vary based on starting weight, adherence to treatment, diet, and exercise. Most patients begin seeing noticeable results within the first 4-8 weeks, with progressive weight loss continuing throughout treatment.
Are there any side effects?
Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation, particularly when starting treatment or increasing dosage. These typically subside within a few weeks as your body adjusts. The Microdosed option is specifically designed to minimize side effects through gradual titration. Serious side effects are rare but can include pancreatitis and gallbladder issues. Our medical team monitors for adverse effects and adjusts treatment as needed.
What is the difference between the three Tirzepatide options?
Microdosed Tirzepatide offers a gentler introduction with gradual dose escalation, ideal for beginners or those sensitive to medications. Sublingual Tirzepatide provides a needle-free alternative with dissolvable tablets placed under the tongue. Tirzepatide Vials offer the traditional injectable format with maximum potency and flexible dosing options. All three formulations contain the same active ingredient with comparable effectiveness.
How is this different from Ozempic or Mounjaro?
Telos Rx offers compounded Tirzepatide (the same active ingredient as Mounjaro/Zepbound) sourced from accredited compounding pharmacies. Tirzepatide is a dual-action medication (GIP and GLP-1) while Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) is GLP-1 only. Studies show Tirzepatide may produce greater weight loss than Semaglutide. Our compounded versions are often more affordable and accessible than brand-name alternatives.
Is there a monthly commitment?
Treatment plans operate on a monthly basis with flexibility to pause, modify, or cancel at any time. Weight loss medications work best with consistent use, so ongoing treatment is recommended for optimal results. Our medical team provides guidance on treatment duration based on your individual goals and progress.
