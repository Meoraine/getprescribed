Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can I expect to lose?
Clinical studies show GLP-1 medications can produce 15-22% body weight reduction over 12-18 months. Individual results vary based on starting weight, adherence to treatment, diet, and exercise. Most patients see noticeable results within the first 4-8 weeks, with progressive weight loss continuing over time.
Are there any side effects?
Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation, especially when starting treatment or increasing dosage. These typically subside within a few weeks as your body adjusts. Serious side effects are rare but can include pancreatitis and gallbladder issues. The medical team monitors for adverse effects and adjusts treatment as needed.
How is this different from Ozempic or Wegovy?
Enhance.md offers compounded versions of Semaglutide (same active ingredient as Ozempic/Wegovy) and Tirzepatide (same as Mounjaro/Zepbound). These are sourced from accredited compounding pharmacies, often at lower cost than brand-name versions. The medications work identically in the body but may be more affordable and accessible.
What is NAD+ therapy and how does it work?
NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell that declines with age. It plays crucial roles in energy metabolism, DNA repair, and cellular signaling. NAD+ therapy aims to restore levels to support healthy aging, improve energy, enhance cognitive function, and support metabolic health. It's often used alongside weight loss treatments for comprehensive wellness.
Is there a monthly commitment?
Treatment plans typically operate on a monthly basis, but there's flexibility to pause or cancel. Weight loss medications work best with consistent use, so ongoing treatment is recommended for optimal results. The medical team provides guidance on treatment duration based on your individual goals and progress.
