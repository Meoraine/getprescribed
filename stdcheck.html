Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I get tested after exposure?
Testing windows vary by STD. For most infections, wait 2 weeks after exposure for accurate results. However, HIV RNA testing can detect infection as early as 9-11 days post-exposure. If you're concerned about recent exposure, consider the 10-Test Panel with HIV RNA Early Detection for the most comprehensive and fastest screening.
What if I test positive?
If you test positive, STDCheck.com provides a free consultation with a licensed physician who can discuss your results, answer questions, and provide treatment options or prescriptions when appropriate. For certain infections, they can even send prescriptions directly to your preferred pharmacy.
Will this show up on my insurance?
No. Since you pay directly without using insurance, there's no reporting to your insurance company, no Explanation of Benefits (EOB) mailed to your home, and nothing appears on your permanent medical record. Your privacy is completely protected.
How accurate are the tests?
STDCheck.com uses FDA-approved tests that are 99% accurate when taken after the appropriate window period. All testing is performed in CLIA-certified laboratories—the same standards required of hospital labs. Accuracy varies slightly by specific test, but all meet or exceed clinical standards.
Can I use my HSA or FSA card?
Yes, STDCheck.com accepts HSA (Health Savings Account) and FSA (Flexible Spending Account) cards. STD testing is an eligible medical expense, so you can use your pre-tax dollars to pay for testing, saving you money.
