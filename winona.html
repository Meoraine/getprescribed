Frequently Asked Questions

What is hormone replacement therapy (HRT)?
Hormone replacement therapy (HRT) involves taking medications containing female hormones to replace the ones your body stops making during menopause. Bioidentical HRT uses plant-derived hormones that are chemically identical to those your body naturally produces. HRT is the most effective treatment for hot flashes and night sweats and can also help prevent bone loss and fractures in postmenopausal women.
Is HRT safe for everyone?
HRT isn't suitable for everyone. Women with a history of certain cancers, blood clots, liver disease, or stroke may need alternative treatments. Winona's doctors conduct thorough health screenings to determine if HRT is appropriate for you. For those who can't take hormones, non-hormonal prescription options are available and can be very effective for symptom management.
How quickly will I see results?
Many women notice improvement in hot flashes and night sweats within 2-4 weeks of starting HRT, with full benefits typically achieved by 3 months. Other symptoms like mood changes and sleep quality may improve gradually. Winona's medical team monitors your progress and adjusts dosages to optimize results while minimizing side effects.
What's the difference between perimenopause and menopause?
Perimenopause is the transition period leading up to menopause, typically starting in your 40s, when hormone levels begin to fluctuate. Symptoms can include irregular periods, hot flashes, and mood changes. Menopause is officially diagnosed after 12 consecutive months without a menstrual period. Winona treats women in all stages—from perimenopause through postmenopause—with tailored treatment approaches for each phase.
Can I use Winona if I'm already on hormone therapy?
Yes, Winona can review your current hormone therapy and optimize your treatment plan. Many women transition to Winona for more personalized care, better pricing, or the convenience of telehealth. The medical team will evaluate your current regimen and make appropriate adjustments based on your symptoms and health goals.
