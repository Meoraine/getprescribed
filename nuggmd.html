Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the medical card last?
Medical cannabis recommendations typically last 1 year, though this varies by state (some are 6 months, others up to 2 years). NuggMD sends renewal reminders before expiration to ensure continuous access. Renewal evaluations are typically faster than initial applications since you're already in the system.
Can I use my medical card in other states?
Some states offer reciprocity, meaning they accept out-of-state medical cards. However, this varies significantly by state. Nevada, Arizona, and Maine are examples of states with reciprocity programs. Always check local laws before traveling with cannabis products. NuggMD can provide state-specific guidance during your consultation.
What if I'm not approved?
NuggMD offers a money-back guarantee. If the doctor determines you don't qualify for medical cannabis, you won't be charged for the evaluation. This makes the process completely risk-free. The doctors are knowledgeable about qualifying conditions and will provide honest assessments.
Will this show up on a background check?
Medical cannabis recommendations are protected health information under HIPAA. They do not appear on standard background checks or employment screenings. However, certain federal positions or jobs requiring security clearances may have different requirements. The recommendation is confidential between you, your doctor, and the state medical cannabis program.
Can I get a card without a serious medical condition?
Qualifying conditions vary by state. Some states have broad definitions that include chronic pain, anxiety, or insomnia—conditions many people experience. The doctors at NuggMD will evaluate your specific symptoms and medical history to determine if you qualify under your state's regulations. It's worth consulting even if you're unsure about eligibility.
